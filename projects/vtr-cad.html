01 / THE PROBLEM

A legal placement is not necessarily a routable placement.

FPGA placement traditionally balances wirelength and timing before detailed routing reveals the most difficult congestion hotspots. If routing demand is discovered too late, the flow must absorb detours, timing degradation, or failed routes. Congestion-aware placement adds an early estimate of routing pressure to the placement objective.

03 / PACKING

Use circuit connectivity before physical placement begins.

In related work, the circuit netlist is modeled as a hypergraph and partitioned with hMETIS. The goal is to create groups with strong internal connectivity and fewer expensive cuts, allowing packing and early placement decisions to reflect the communication structure of the design.

Together, these experiments provided hands-on experience with the complete VTR flow—from logical clusters to placement objectives and downstream routing behavior.
