01 / ABOUT

Researching computer systems as a connected whole.

My work spans processor microarchitecture, operating systems, FPGA acceleration, and design automation. I am especially interested in the boundaries where abstractions become overhead—and where hardware/software co-design can remove it.

My M.A.Sc. thesis introduces MEERKAT, a Linux-capable FPGA research framework built on CVA5. It treats hardware accelerators as first-class compute elements and studies the communication, invocation, interrupt, and scheduling costs that operating systems impose.
